Hi, I’m Zhiqing.

I study how AI can support people
in the middle of what they’re doing.

I’m a PhD student advised by Steven Dow. I build and study interactive systems for collaboration, with a focus on when and how AI should intervene.

My projects explore AI support for small groups, from children creating art together to participants making sense of a topic in conversation.

Ongoing research

What I’m working on now

I’m exploring how proactive AI can offer support at the right moment, within conversations and across complex tasks.

CONVERSATIONAL INTERVENTIONS

When and how should AI enter a conversation?

I design visual and audio interventions with different levels of intensity, using turn-taking models and probe-based interviews to understand how people respond to an AI facilitator entering a group discussion.

With Steven Dow · UC San Diego Design Lab

AI-ASSISTED MULTITASKING

Supporting attention across tasks and agents.

I analyze human–AI coding conversations to understand how developers coordinate multiple AI agents and long-running tasks, and where proactive support could reduce the effort of managing these workflows.

With Scott Klemmer · UC San Diego

AI SUPPORT IN COLLABORATIVE LEARNING

Helping a group move an idea forward.

In AskArt, I studied how children use generative AI during group art projects. The design challenge was to make AI useful within their shared activity: helping them ask questions, explore ideas, and move between talking and making.

02

PROACTIVE AI FACILITATION

Knowing when to step into the discussion.

With PeerFocus, I study the boundary between a useful cue and an interruption. How can AI help a group explore a topic while leaving participants room to guide the conversation?

MANUSCRIPT UNDER REVIEW · CHI 2027

PeerFocus

I designed, built, and deployed a mixed-initiative facilitator combining structured agendas and LLM-generated cues with participant-led discussion. A study with 88 participants across 33 sessions examined topic transitions, cue engagement, peer interaction, and insight quality.
